# Contributing

Aion is a non-commercial project and it is better with more eyes. You do not need to be an expert, a developer or a historian. Curiosity is enough.

## Ways to help

**Tell us about a mistake.** A wrong date, a broken link, a source that does not say what the page claims, a place on the wrong spot. These are the most valuable messages we get, and each one is checked against the source and answered.

**Suggest a place.** A site, a find, an event or a route that is missing. What makes a suggestion easy to use:

1. The name, and any other names it goes by.
2. Where it is (a coordinate, or a place name and country).
3. One or more dates, each with **a link to a source that can be opened** (an excavation report, a heritage listing, a museum page, a paper).
4. A sentence on why it matters, or what makes the dating uncertain.

You do not have to get the grade right. We assign it from the sources.

**Tell us where good data lives.** Open datasets, university projects, national heritage portals, museum collections with open licences, historical map projects. If you know of one we do not use, a link is enough.

**Suggest a layer.** What question would you like to ask a globe like this? Trade routes, languages, plagues, shipwrecks, megaliths, ancient observatories, water systems, the spread of crops. Say what you would want to see and what data might exist.

**Help with the practical side.** Testing on different devices, accessibility, translations, data cleaning, and comments from teachers on what would work in a classroom.

## How to reach us

Use the **[contact form](https://aion.eanna.house/site/contact.html)** on the globe. It goes to the person who runs the site. Please write in English or Dutch.

## What happens next

1. A person or an agent reads it and checks the sources you gave.
2. If it is a correction, it is made, and checked by a second reviewer. If it is a new place, it is researched against the source tiers ([Evidence and sources](evidence-and-sources.md)).
3. If we use your idea or data, we will credit you or your source if you want that.
4. If we cannot use it, we will usually say why.

We cannot promise a time. This is a spare-time project and quality matters more than speed.

## What we cannot accept

- Claims that cannot be sourced, however plausible. They can stay as "speculative" only if a source for the hypothesis exists and the label says so.
- Photos or text copied from sources without a clear licence.
- Commercial or promotional material. The project has no business model and does not link to sellers.
- Anything that ranks traditions or peoples against each other.
